Roadster template

A reusable template in the style of the Roadster dossier. Fill in one JSON file and get a PDF.

  • Cover with a large photo and key figures
  • Labels such as OFFICIAL, CLAIM and ESTIMATE
  • Tables, boxes, timeline and numbered sources
  • Dutch and English
  • Download the manual (PDF, Dutch)
  • Download the blank example (PDF, English)
  • Download the template (zip)

Requires Python 3 and Google Chrome or Chromium. As of October 2026.
