What the footage tells you

How to get a useful analysis

Film one rider, with the whole body visible and good contrast. Use a stationary camera from the side for knee angles. Choose a short passage around one attempt.

This model estimates human joints, not board or foil position. Values are 2D image measurements, not joint diagnostics, foot pressure, speed or a technique score. A change of camera angle changes the numbers.

If tracking fails, use playback and place four checkpoints yourself. Manual inspection is not AI analysis.

Method & sources
